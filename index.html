How it works

Fees go in the can. Supply comes out smaller.

No staking, no claiming, nothing to sign. Holding is the whole job.

1→

Trades pay fees

Every buy and sell of $SCRAP pays pump.fun creator fees, on the bonding curve and on PumpSwap after graduation.

2→

Every 3 minutes

The engine claims all of the fees that piled up. Dust rolls into the next dive instead of wasting a transaction.

3→

One market buy

All of it goes into a single buy of $SCRAP on whichever venue the coin trades on: curve, PumpSwap, or Jupiter as a fallback.

4

Burned

Exactly what that buy brought in is burned on-chain. The engine never sells and never touches tokens it did not buy.

FAQ

Questions from the dumpster.

Did an AI really come up with this?

Yes. The name, the mascot, the tagline and the rule set come from a single AI answer to a single prompt. Humans drew the sticker, wrote the engine and pressed launch.

Where do the burned tokens come from?

From the market. The engine claims the pump.fun creator fees, spends them on one buy of $SCRAP and burns the tokens that buy returned. Nothing is burned from a pre-minted stash.

Can the engine sell or rug?

The engine has no sell path. The code is public, so you can check: it claims, buys and burns. It only burns the difference between its balance before and after its own buy.

What happens when volume is low?

Small pots roll over. If the fees are under the minimum, the dive is skipped and the fees wait for the next one. Burns scale with volume, there is no fixed promise.

Can I run the engine for my own coin?

Yes. Clone the repo, set TOKEN_MINT and a wallet key in engine/.env and start it. Without a key it runs in watch mode and only simulates.
